# Sacramento STR legislative history

Summary: Documented Sacramento’s short-term rental legislative history from 2016 onward (primary framework Ords. 2016-0003/2016-0004), later amendments (2018-0040, 2020-0005, 2020-0025 multi-unit CUP pathway, 2024-0007 district/platform assessments, 2024-0051 Title 17 conformance), Airbnb municipal TOT collection beginning 2017-09-01, and Airbnb City Portal data/compliance connection as a Sept. 2020 pilot city. Updated `str_regulations.json` for Sacramento with `agent_checked: 1`.

## City processed

- **Sacramento, CA** (first list item lacking `agent_checked`)

## Main findings

### Primary framework (2016)

- **Ord. 2016-0004** (adopted 2016-01-19, effective ~2016-02-18) added **Chapter 5.114**: annual STR permit, primary vs. non-primary (90-day) rules, six-guest occupancy, host compliance, fees/penalties, and TOT/BOT duties.
- **Ord. 2016-0003** same day amended **Title 17 / §17.228.104** so qualifying STRs are a permitted dwelling use without a B&B CUP.

### Later binding amendments

| Ordinance / action | Dates | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ord. 2018-0040 | Passed 2018-08-14; effective 2018-09-13 | Multi-permit / lot occupancy / CC&Rs / illegal advertising |
| Res. 2018-0419 (STID) | 2018-10-30 | 1% tourism infrastructure assessment on short-term stays |
| Ord. 2020-0005 | Passed 2020-02-04; effective 2020-03-05 | Article V hosting-platform duties (permit gating; TOT operator) |
| Ord. 2020-0025 | Enacted July 2020 (staff); month-level dates used | Multi-unit CUP pathway in listed zones |
| Ord. 2024-0007 | Passed 2024-04-30; effective 2024-05-30 | Platform collection of STMD/STID; 90-day clarification |
| Ord. 2024-0051 | Passed 2024-12-10; effective 2025-01-09 | Conforming Title 17 STR definition |

Proposed 2025–2026 primary-residence / platform-reporting overhaul (File IDs 2025-01007 / 2025-01885) was **not** included because it had not been adopted as binding law as of this research.

### Airbnb tax and data

- **TOT collection:** **2017-09-01** — Airbnb Host Community notice plus City Finance staff reports confirming September 2017 start of municipal 12% TOT remittance.
- **Data sharing:** **2020-09-23** — Airbnb City Portal launch listing Sacramento as a pilot; Mayor Steinberg quote; later Airbnb/GovTech confirm registration enforcement and listing action via the Portal.

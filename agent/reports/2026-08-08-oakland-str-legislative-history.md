# Oakland STR legislative history

Summary: Documented Oakland’s short-term rental legislative history from 2009 onward. Oakland still has no dedicated STR permit ordinance (draft STRR rules remain pending; adoption hearings targeted Fall 2026). Binding regulation is the Planning Code’s 30-day Permanent Residential / Transient Habitation framework (primary: Ord. 13172 C.M.S., 2013), plus the 2009 B&B classification (Ord. 12939), Measure C 14% TOT (2010), the 2017 Secondary Unit/ADU 30-day occupancy ban (Ord. 13435), 2018 Transient Habitation Major CUP rules (Ord. 13501), and 2024 Chapter 17.10 definition clean-ups (Ord. 13779). Airbnb municipal TOT collection began in 2015; no Airbnb–City listing/compliance data connection was identified. Updated `str_regulations.json` for Oakland with `agent_checked: 1`.

## Main findings

### De facto framework (no dedicated STR ordinance)

- City and Oaklandside reporting confirm residential rentals under 30 days are generally unlawful as ordinary residential use; ~2,500 Airbnb listings have operated without a legal STR pathway while the City still collects TOT.
- Resolution No. 86298 C.M.S. (2016) directed staff to develop STRR policy options but is not binding regulation and was **not** included.
- Proposed 2023–2026 STRR licensing/90-night/primary-residence rules and Granicus implementation work were **not** included because they had not been adopted as binding law.

### Binding actions included

| Ordinance / action | Dates | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ord. 12939 C.M.S. | Passed 2009-06-16; effective 2009-07-16 | Codified B&B Residential Activities (then less-than-weekly) vs Transient Habitation |
| Measure C (OMC §4.24.031) | Voters 2009-07-21; effective 2010-01-01 | Raised TOT from 11% to 14% |
| Ord. 13172 C.M.S. | Passed 2013-07-02; effective 2013-08-01 | **Primary framework:** shifted occupancy thresholds to 30 days |
| Ord. 13435 C.M.S. | Passed 2017-05-02; effective 2017-06-01 | Secondary Unit/ADU occupancy 30+ days only |
| Ord. 13501 C.M.S. | Passed 2018-07-24; effective 2018-08-23 | Major CUP for Transient Habitation (hotel) use |
| Ord. 13779 C.M.S. | Passed 2024-01-16; effective 2024-02-15 | Revised Permanent Residential / Semi-Transient definitions |

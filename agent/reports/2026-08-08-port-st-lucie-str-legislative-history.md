# Port St. Lucie, FL — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Port St. Lucie has no city-specific short-term rental ordinance. Airbnb-style rentals are governed by Florida’s vacation-rental licensing and local-regulation preemption under **Ch. 2011-119 (HB 883)** and the narrowed **Ch. 2014-71 (SB 356)** rule in s. 509.032(7)(b), F.S., plus generally applicable city business-tax, zoning, building, and nuisance codes. **Ch. 2025-190 (SB 180)** freezes more restrictive local land-development rules through October 1, 2027 (St. Lucie County is in the Hurricane Milton federal disaster declaration). Airbnb collects state sales/transient tax statewide but does **not** collect St. Lucie County Tourist Development Tax; no City–Airbnb compliance data connection was found.

## Main findings

1. **No city STR registry or land-use STR code.** Port St. Lucie’s Code of Ordinances (Municode) and secondary code reviews show no vacation-rental / short-term-rental permit, registration, duration, or frequency ordinance. Operators need Florida DBPR vacation-rental licensing (where ch. 509 applies) and a City Business Tax Receipt under Chapter 111; neighboring Fort Pierce and St. Lucie Village registration programs do not apply inside Port St. Lucie.

2. **State preemption is the primary framework.** Ch. 2011-119 (eff. 2011-06-02) and Ch. 2014-71 (eff. 2014-07-01) bar local prohibition or duration/frequency regulation of vacation rentals except pre–June 1, 2011 ordinances. Port St. Lucie had no such grandfathered STR ordinance. CS/SB 280 (2024), which would have authorized local registration programs and platform listing/tax rules, was vetoed and is not binding law.

3. **SB 180 LDR freeze.** Ch. 2025-190 constrains more restrictive local land-development rules through 2027 for covered jurisdictions; St. Lucie County (and thus Port St. Lucie) is listed in FEMA-4834-DR (Hurricane Milton).

4. **Airbnb tax collection:** **null** — No municipal TOT. County TDT (5%) is host-remitted per the St. Lucie County Tax Collector; Airbnb Help Article 2301 does not list St. Lucie among counties where Airbnb collects TDT. Statewide state sales/transient tax collection does not qualify.

5. **Airbnb data sharing:** **null** — No City of Port St. Lucie–Airbnb City Portal, listing API, or enforcement data connection found.

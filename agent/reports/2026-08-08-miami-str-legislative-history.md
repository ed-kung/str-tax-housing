# Miami, FL — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Miami’s primary STR framework is **Ordinance 13114 (Miami 21)**, adopted 2009-10-22 and effective **2010-05-20**, which limits T3 to predominantly permanent housing and treats under-one-month multifamily rentals as lodging—grandfathered under Florida’s June 1, 2011 vacation-rental preemption. State Ch. 2011-119 and Ch. 2014-71 set that preemption; *City of Miami v. Airbnb* (2018) allowed Miami 21’s text to stand while rejecting the City’s overbroad 2015 categorical ban. Dedicated city STR registration ordinances were never adopted. Airbnb began collecting Miami-Dade’s 6% tourist/resort taxes for City of Miami stays on **2017-05-01**; no City of Miami–Airbnb data connection was found (City Portal pilot was unincorporated Miami-Dade only).

## Main findings

1. **Miami 21 (Ord. 13114)** is the city’s primary STR framework: T3 residential = predominantly permanent housing; lodging not permitted in T3; under-one-month multifamily rentals treated as lodging. Effective 2010-05-20 (postponed from 2010-02-19).

2. **No dedicated city STR ordinance enacted.** Proposed registration/permit schemes (File 1661; Art. 6.3.6 / 7.1.2.10) were withdrawn or never received final action. March 2017 Commission resolution directed enforcement of existing zoning (not new binding law). Zoning Interpretation 2015-001 was administrative and partly preempted.

3. **State preemption.** Ch. 2011-119 (eff. 2011-06-02) and Ch. 2014-71 (eff. 2014-07-01) bar local prohibition or duration/frequency regulation of vacation rentals except pre-6/1/2011 ordinances—preserving Miami 21. *City of Miami v. Airbnb*, 260 So. 3d 478 (Fla. 3d DCA 2018) vacated an overbroad injunction: Miami 21 bars STRs that convert T3 properties away from predominantly permanent housing, but a categorical under-one-month ban beyond that text is preempted.

4. **Ch. 2025-190 (SB 180)** freezes more restrictive local land-development rules through 2027 for covered jurisdictions including Miami-Dade.

5. **Airbnb tax collection:** **2017-05-01** — Miami-Dade 6% tourist/resort tax VCA (County approval 2017-04-04); covers City of Miami (no separate city resort tax). State sales tax collection earlier (2015) not recorded.

6. **Airbnb data sharing:** **null** — City Portal pilot listed unincorporated Miami-Dade, not City of Miami; 2017 tax MOU did not include host identity sharing.

# Virginia Beach, VA — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Virginia Beach’s primary STR zoning framework is **Ordinance No. 3578** (adopted 2019-01-15; effective/enforced **2019-11-01**), which created home-sharing vs. short-term rental definitions and § 241.2 operational rules. Later **Ord. 3668 (2021-07-13)** confined new STRs to Sandbridge and petitioned/council STR overlays, with **Ord. 3673 (2021-09-07)** reopening the Oceanfront Resort via CUP plus citywide safety inspections. State law shaped Sandbridge (HB 824, 2018) and platform tax/reporting (HB 518/SB 651, effective **2022-10-01**). Airbnb municipal TOT collection and monthly address/gross-receipts reporting for Virginia Beach begin **2022-10-01**.

## Main findings

1. **2016 tax registration.** On 2016-08-16 Council required online/short-term lodging operators to register as TOT trustees with the Commissioner of the Revenue and added failure-to-register penalties; TOT already applied, but platform hosts had largely not complied.

2. **State enabling / Sandbridge.** SB 1578 (Ch. 741; eff. 2017-07-01) authorized local STR registries. HB 824 (Ch. 758; eff. 2018-07-01) mandated Sandbridge SSD STRs as a principal use.

3. **Ord. 3578 (primary framework).** Adopted 2019-01-15; effective/enforced 2019-11-01. CUP pathway for new non-home-sharing STRs; grandfathering for pre-2018-07-01 registrants; occupancy, contract-frequency, parking, insurance, and nuisance rules.

4. **2020–2021 enforcement geography.** Ord. 3647 (2020-09-15) moved STR zoning violations to civil penalties. Ord. 3668 (2021-07-13) created Article 23 overlays and largely banned new STRs outside Sandbridge/overlays. Ord. 3673 + companions (2021-09-07) added Oceanfront Resort STR Overlay (CUP) and life-safety/deck inspection rules.

5. **Platform taxes & data (2022-10-01).** HB 518/SB 651 require intermediaries to collect/remit local TOT and file monthly address/gross-receipts reports with localities. Airbnb previously collected state sales tax only for Virginia Beach listings.

6. **2025 task force package.** Ords. 3818 & 3821 (2025-08-12) restored criminal misdemeanor penalties and adjusted parking, signage, fees, and inspections.

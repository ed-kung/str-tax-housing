# Omaha, NE — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Omaha has no dedicated city short-term-rental licensing ordinance. Binding STR policy for the city is set by Nebraska LB 57 (2019) (Neb. Rev. Stat. § 18-1758), which bars local STR bans and limits STR-specific regulation to health/safety and taxes, and by LB 284 (2019), which requires marketplace facilitators such as Airbnb to collect/remit state and local sales taxes and state/county lodging taxes on facilitated stays. Airbnb began collecting Nebraska taxes in September 2019 and currently also collects Omaha’s 5.5% Transient Rental Guest Tax; no city–Airbnb compliance data feed was identified. As of mid-2026, City Council proposals for STR registration remain pending and are not yet law.

## Main findings

1. **No binding city STR framework (as of research date).** Local news (WOWT/KETV, 2026) and councilmember statements describe Omaha as lacking STR registration/licensing; a proposed registration/emergency-contact ordinance had not been enacted. Secondary sites claiming a general “Chapter 43, Article XI” Airbnb licensing regime appear to conflate Omaha’s bed-and-breakfast license article with modern STR rules; 2015 rooming-house legislation that would have swept in Airbnb was amended to remove that language before passage.

2. **LB 57 (approved 2019-03-07; operative 2019-09-06)** is the primary framework: prohibits municipal STR bans; cabins STR-specific local rules to health/safety and emergency contacts; allows sales/occupation taxes; requires equal land-use/nuisance/property-maintenance treatment; authorized (later largely superseded) DOR–platform tax agreements.

3. **LB 284 (approved 2019-03-21; operative 2019-04-01)** requires marketplace facilitators meeting nexus thresholds to collect Nebraska and local sales taxes and, per DOR GIL 1-19-1, state and county lodging taxes on facilitated short-term lodging—covering Omaha city sales tax and Douglas County lodging tax on platform bookings.

4. **Airbnb tax collection:** First known municipal-relevant collection date **2019-09-01**, based on Airbnb’s announcement that Nebraska state sales and county lodging collection began in September 2019 (local sales tax remitted with state sales under LB 284). Airbnb help article 2625 also documents collection of Omaha’s 5.5% Transient Rental Guest Tax (city occupation tax); earliest Wayback confirmation of that TRGT listing is 2021-10-19.

5. **Airbnb data sharing:** `null` — no official city or Airbnb announcement of an API, automated listing feed, or enforcement portal integration with Omaha.

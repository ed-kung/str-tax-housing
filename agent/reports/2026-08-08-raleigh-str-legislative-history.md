# Raleigh, NC — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Raleigh’s current STR framework is Ordinance (2021) 193 TC 444 (TC-8-20), effective 2021-03-04 with compliance required 2021-06-02, which repealed the never-enforced 2019 Homestay rules and legalized whole-home and partial-unit STRs with an annual zoning permit. Earlier UDO overnight-lodging rules made residential STRs technically illegal but were under a multi-year enforcement stay after late 2014. State rental registration/permit preemption (S.L. 2011-281 → 2016-122 → 2019-73 → 160D-1207) constrains registry-style rules. Airbnb began collecting Wake County occupancy tax for Raleigh stays on **2015-06-01** and named Raleigh a City Portal pilot on **2020-09-23**.

## Main findings

1. **Pre-2021 limbo.** UDO (2013) treated under-30-day dwelling rentals as overnight lodging not allowed in ordinary residential districts. After a late-2014 Airbnb cease-and-desist, Council stayed STR enforcement while studying rules; whole-house rentals operated for years without active zoning enforcement.

2. **TC-1-19 Homestay (adopted 2019-05-21; effective 2019-08-19)** allowed only owner-occupied room rentals with host presence and permits, and Council scheduled whole-house ban enforcement for 2020-01-01. The new Council stayed both on 2019-12-10; news/city statements confirm the 2019 package was never enforced before repeal. `enforcement_date`: null.

3. **TC-8-20 (adopted 2021-02-02)** is the primary framework: Limited Use STR in listed districts; whole or partial dwelling; zoning permit; ad/on-site permit posting; multi-unit cap; nuisance/revocation pathway. Compliance required 90 days after 2021-03-04 effective date → **2021-06-02**. TC-3-22 (2022) clarified “miscellaneous zoning permit.”

4. **State preemption** (S.L. 2011-281, 2016-122, 2019-73, Chapter 160D-1207 via S.L. 2019-111/2020-25) bars general rental registration; *Schroeder* (2022) affirmed zoning-style STR standards remain available—consistent with Raleigh’s zoning-permit approach.

5. **Airbnb tax collection:** **2015-06-01** — Wake County 6% Room Occupancy Tax (+ NC sales tax) under the NC VCA; Raleigh has no separate city occupancy tax.

6. **Airbnb data sharing:** **2020-09-23** — Airbnb City Portal launch listing Raleigh as a first-phase pilot.

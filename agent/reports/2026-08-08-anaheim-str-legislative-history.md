# Anaheim, CA — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Anaheim’s first dedicated STR permit program was **Ordinance 6299** (May 13, 2014). After a 2015–2017 urgency moratorium on new permits (**Ord. 6343** as extended), **Ord. 6374** (eff. Aug. 11, 2016) banned new STRs and added platform notice-and-takedown duties, while **Ord. 6375** amortized most existing STRs over 18 months. Hardship/settlement amendments followed in 2017; **Ord. 6466** (June 2019) ended the phase-out for most qualifying pre-moratorium permits while keeping the new-STR ban—the current closed-permit framework. **Measure J / Ord. 6549** (Nov.–Dec. 2022) made online travel companies TOT “operators,” including for STR bookings. Airbnb municipal 15% TOT collection is documented from early 2023 (**2023-01-01** proxy); no City Portal / direct compliance data connection found.

## Main findings

1. **Primary framework is municipal Chapter 4.05.** Ord. 6299 created the permit program (3-night minimum, occupancy/parking/nuisance rules, TOT). Ord. 6374 closed the market to new residential STRs and imposed hosting-platform listing removal duties. Ord. 6466 restored ongoing operation for most remaining grandfathered permits under good-neighbor rules; new STRs remain prohibited (limited HOA-ban relocation historically noted in City fact sheets).

2. **2016 amortization was enforced, then largely reversed.** Ord. 6375’s default February 11, 2018 wind-down and hardship process were administered (with 2017 settlement revisions). June 2019 Ord. 6466 ended amortization for Qualifying Pre-Moratorium / home-share / specified CC&R-restricted STRs.

3. **TOT / platforms.** Hosts have long owed Anaheim’s 15% TOT. Measure J (voter-approved Nov. 8, 2022; Ord. 6549 Dec. 6, 2022) expressly requires OTCs collecting STR rent to remit full TOT. Airbnb’s CA tax help page omitted Anaheim as of Dec. 4, 2022 and listed 15% collection by Feb. 4, 2023.

4. **Airbnb data sharing:** **null** — no City Portal or SB 346 local activating ordinance identified; §4.05.120 is notice-and-takedown only.

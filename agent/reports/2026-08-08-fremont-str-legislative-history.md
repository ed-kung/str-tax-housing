# Fremont, CA — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Fremont had no dedicated Airbnb-era STR ordinance until **Ordinance No. 01-2024** (adopted Jan. 9, 2024; effective Feb. 8, 2024), which added **FMC Chapter 5.100**. Existing operators had until **Dec. 31, 2024** to apply for a permit; full enforcement against unpermitted pre-existing STRs is treated as **Jan. 1, 2025**. No confirmed Airbnb municipal TOT collection or direct compliance data connection found.

## Main findings

1. **Primary STR framework is municipal Chapter 5.100 (Ord. 01-2024).** City Council hearing Dec. 12, 2023; second reading/adoption Jan. 9, 2024. Implements Housing Element Program No. 9. Requires an STR permit; limits STRs to the host’s primary dwelling/primary residence; bars ADUs/JADUs, accessory structures, RVs, SB 9 units, and affordable units; one STR per host / one concurrent per dwelling; no annual night cap; two guests per bedroom; 24/7 local contact (60-minute response); Good Neighbor / neighbor-notice / insurance / life-safety rules; commercial event ban; elevated health/safety fines; platforms largely carved out of advertising prohibition.

2. **Phase-in / amnesty.** Effective Feb. 8, 2024 (30 days after adoption; confirmed by City news release). Existing STRs with a business tax ID had until Dec. 31, 2024 to apply; thereafter unpermitted existing hosts must cease. Enforcement date recorded as **2025-01-01**.

3. **Pre-2024 baseline.** No earlier city STR-specific permitting ordinance identified from 2008 onward. General hotel TOT (FMC Ch. 3.10 / Ord. 453 lineage, 10%) already applied to transient stays but is not STR-specific legislation post-2008.

4. **Airbnb tax collection:** **null** — Airbnb help article 2297 does not list Fremont for municipal TOT remittance (nearby Alameda/Berkeley/Union City are listed). City materials put remittance on hosts.

5. **Airbnb data sharing:** **null** — No City Portal / API / confirmed direct feed. No Fremont SB 346 activating ordinance identified.

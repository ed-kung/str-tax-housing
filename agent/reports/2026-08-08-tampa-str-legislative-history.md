# Tampa, FL — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Tampa has no dedicated city STR ordinance and no pre–June 1, 2011 grandfathered vacation-rental code. Binding regulation is state law: **Ch. 2011-119** (vacation-rental licensing + broad preemption) and **Ch. 2014-71** (current s. 509.032(7)(b) ban on local prohibition or duration/frequency rules). **Ch. 2025-190 (SB 180)** further freezes more restrictive local land-development rules through October 1, 2027 (Hillsborough was in Helene/Milton disaster declarations). Airbnb began collecting Hillsborough County Tourist Development Tax for Tampa stays on bookings effective **2016-12-20**; no City of Tampa–Airbnb compliance data connection was found (Visit Tampa Bay’s City Portal access is tourism insights only).

## Main findings

1. **No city STR framework enacted.** Tampa never adopted a dedicated short-term/vacation-rental registration or permit ordinance. Hosts rely on Florida DBPR vacation-rental licensing, City of Tampa Business Tax Receipt (Ch. 24), and generally applicable zoning/noise/building codes. A separate residential rental-certificate/landlord registry (not STR-specific) was repealed effective May 4, 2023 and is not recorded as STR legislation.

2. **State preemption governs.** Ch. 2011-119 (eff. 2011-06-02) and Ch. 2014-71 (eff. 2014-07-01) bar local prohibition or duration/frequency regulation of vacation rentals except pre-6/1/2011 ordinances—Tampa has none. Hillsborough County’s August 2026 draft STR registration ordinance (proposed for unincorporated areas; hearing Sept. 2, 2026) is not yet law and would not apply inside Tampa.

3. **Ch. 2025-190 (SB 180)** freezes more restrictive local land-development rules through 2027 for covered jurisdictions including Hillsborough/Tampa.

4. **Airbnb tax collection:** **2016-12-20** — Hillsborough County Tax Collector lists Airbnb as collecting/remitting county Tourist Development Tax for bookings effective that date (covers City of Tampa; no separate city TOT). News coverage reported the VCA contractually effective February 1, 2017. State sales tax alone is not recorded.

5. **Airbnb data sharing:** **null** — Visit Tampa Bay was a 2020 City Portal tourism pilot partner; no City of Tampa listing-level compliance/enforcement data connection found.

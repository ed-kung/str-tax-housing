# Chula Vista, CA — Short-term rental legislative history

**Summary:** Chula Vista had no dedicated STR ordinance until **Ordinance No. 3514** (adopted Dec. 14, 2021), which added **CVMC Chapter 5.68**. Operational rules began **Feb. 1, 2022**; permits and hosting-platform duties (including TOT collection and reporting) began **July 1, 2022**. Companion **Resolution No. 2021-245** added STR permit fees and a Code Enforcement Officer II. Airbnb municipal TOT collection is recorded as **2022-07-01**. No confirmed Airbnb City Portal / direct compliance data connection found.

## Main findings

1. **Primary STR framework is municipal Chapter 5.68 (Ord. 3514).** Alternative ordinance (community-input version) placed on first reading Dec. 7, 2021; adopted Dec. 14, 2021. Caps each resident at two STRs (primary + one non-primary), 275-day primary-residence rule, 90-day primary whole-home cap, two-night minimum for non-primary whole-home stays, ADU/JADU (post-1/1/2020 permit) and affordable-unit bans, occupancy/nuisance/Good Neighbor rules, and platform TOT/reporting/takedown duties.

2. **Phase-in.** CVMC 5.68.220: operational requirements and penalties effective Feb. 1, 2022; permit, renewal, event-permit, and hosting-platform sections effective July 1, 2022. Local news (ABC 10News; Chula Vista Today) confirmed Feb. 1 operational start and July permit enforcement.

3. **Fees/staffing.** Resolution 2021-245 (same night) amended the Master Fee Schedule for STR permit fees and added 1.0 Code Enforcement Officer II.

4. **Pre-2021 baseline.** Ordinance findings state STRs already owed City TOT under CVMC Chapter 3.40, but only ~125 of ~350 listings were remitting as of Oct. 2021; no earlier STR-specific permitting ordinance was identified from 2008 onward.

5. **Airbnb tax collection:** **2022-07-01** — platform TOT duties under 5.68.150(C)/5.68.220; Airbnb help article 2297 lists Chula Vista 10% municipal TOT (absent in Oct. 2021 Wayback capture; present by Dec. 2022).

6. **Airbnb data sharing:** **null** — ordinance creates platform reporting/takedown duties, but City Portal / confirmed direct Airbnb feed not found; city uses Host Compliance/Deckard for listing monitoring.
